❓ How to Play
  • Arrow keys (or the on-screen buttons) move and rotate the falling piece.
  • Down arrow soft-drops, Space hard-drops it instantly for bonus points.
  • Fill a horizontal line completely to clear it — clear 4 at once for a big bonus.
  • Marathon never ends — it just gets faster. Sprint ends the instant you clear 40 lines.
  • The game ends if the stack reaches the top.
